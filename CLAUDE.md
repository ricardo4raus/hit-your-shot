# Hit Your Shot: Idle Sports

Mobile sports shooter with idle/upgrade progression: aim a shooting line, shoot balls at a hole, basket or goal in short timed rounds, earn money per hit, upgrade accuracy, fire rate and income. Unlock more sports, heavier balls and characters with more arms.

Status: idea approved, no code yet.

## Key files

- `docs/game-design.md`: the game. Part 1 is approved by the owner. Part 2 lists open decisions.

## Rules

- Build only what is in Part 1 of `docs/game-design.md`. Ask the owner before building anything from Part 2.
- When the owner decides an open item, move it from Part 2 to Part 1.
- Never mention the multi-arm characters in the game name, icon or first store screenshot. They are a surprise.
- Follow the portfolio layout in `../README.md` (docs/, balance/, tools/, src/). Shared code goes in `../_shared/` later.
